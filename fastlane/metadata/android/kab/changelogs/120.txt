• Tabdart yettwaxedmen seg tezlit itteddun tkemmel yid-s, war ma tales seg tazwara.
• Awiǧit amaynut i yal teɣzi, s wul; mi ara yimɣur, taɣlaft neɣ tamnaḍt n O'rama.
• Tizlatin yexsren: anadi, ticraḍ deg ugar n yimukan, Ṣeggem deg ugar n wumuɣen; aṣeggem yif.
• Tabdart n usekcem yettwasqedcen deg ufella n Tibdarin; ikaramen s tiɣlafin, ula d aẓeṭṭa.
• Agdil n usekkeṛ: tugna-s seg tezlit itteddun, d Attacca nnig-s mi ara yettwaurar uẓawan.
• Tẓa n tutlayin nniḍen: Tahindit, Takannadat, Taɛrabt, Tamaziɣt, Taqbaylit, Taswahilit, Tahawsat, Tayurubat, Tamharit.
