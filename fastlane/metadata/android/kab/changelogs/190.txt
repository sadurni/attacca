• Art-tacca: tacna tseɣbel tugna mi ara teddu, s yini n waman, s zzit, s lmidad neɣ s ukalam n tbuleṭ, seg tsarut-is, ikurden-is, tiyitin-is d teɣlaft-is.
• Neɣ tseɣbel taɣlaft n walbum s timmad-is, aḥric s uḥric (aḍebsi n vinyl ma ulac-itt).
• Yal tugna s uzmul-is, tettwasekles deg ujerrar (Pictures/Attacca), i usemɣer.
• Art-tacca ɣer yidis n O'rama, World neɣ i sin, d ugilal yettembiwilen.
• Tazwara yefsusen uqbel aẓawan tettwaḥsab d tasusmi deg usefsi amyeddi.
