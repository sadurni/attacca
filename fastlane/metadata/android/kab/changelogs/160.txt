• Tibdarin timaynutin: tazmert yettwasunen s ufus, i sin, ayen tesleḍ deg usrag-a, yiwet n tmeẓla, inigen s yimeḥbas, i yiṭes d usaki.
• Asedduy s ufus s yimaɣnuten i yal tabdart; tibdarin war taggara qebblent Ugar am wa d Drus am wa.
• Tizlatin n live s yimesli-nsent: yal yiwet, war live neɣ ala live; yiwet n tezlit seg waṭas n yidisken, yiwet n tikkelt.
• Asefsi amyeddi war tilmi ɣer tezlatin Ogg, Opus neɣ i yebdan s tsusmi.
• Yiwen uẓawan deg tiliɣri nniḍen: kter tamkarḍit-is, war asnirem.
