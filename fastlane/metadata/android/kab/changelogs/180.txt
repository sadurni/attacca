• Taɣara n Attacca World (Iɣewwaṛen → Udem): Tagejdant, Tafellayt neɣ Tafellayt maḍi. Tafellayt maḍi tesbanay-d deg waman akal, aɣerrabu d yiɣersiwen.
• S taɣara tafellayt, akal qrib n tiṭ yesɛa aẓeṭṭa-s: tuga, ijdi, azru, adfel.
• Deg Tfellayt d Tfellayt maḍi, tuga tettmeɣɣi ɣer yidis n webrid, tettḥerrik s waḍu.
• Ṣṣbeɣ d wuzzal ttberriqen deg tafukt, taḍut n yiɣersiwen tettaṭṭaf tafat.
• Initen n usaru deg Attacca World zemren ad remden neɣ ad nsen weḥd-sen.
