• Jagoran sauti (Sauti → Daidaita sauti mataki-mataki): ana jin kowane tasiri A/B/C a kan kiɗanka, don kowace na'ura.
• Jagorori da kunne don sauye-sauye da ƙarar sauti, da dubawar abin da zai iya zama mafi kyau (Saituna).
• Haɗewar shuɗewa kai tsaye ba ta ƙara tsayar da waƙar da aka yanke a tsakiyar sautinta farat ɗaya.
