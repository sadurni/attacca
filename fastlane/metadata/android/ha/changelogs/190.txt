• Art-tacca: waƙar tana zana hoto yayin da take kunnawa, da fentin ruwa, fentin mai, tawada ko biro, daga sautinta, kod ɗinta, bugunta da hoton kundinta.
• Ko ta zana hoton kundin da kansa, taɓi bayan taɓi (faifan garmaho idan babu).
• Kowane hoto da sa hannu, ana ajiye shi a aljihun tebur (Pictures/Attacca), don zuƙowa.
• Art-tacca gefen O'rama, World ko duka biyun, da kuma fuskar bango mai rai.
• Farko mai rauni kafin kiɗa ana ɗaukarsa shiru wajen haɗewa.
