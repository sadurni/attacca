• Attacca World's quality (Settings → Appearance): Basic, High or Maximum. Maximum mirrors the land, the traveller and the animals in the water.
• In high quality the ground has its own grain near the eye: grass, sand, rock, snow.
• In High and Maximum, grass grows by the path and sways in the wind.
• Paint and metal shine in the sun, and the animals' coats catch the light.
• Film tones in Attacca World can be turned on or off on their own.
